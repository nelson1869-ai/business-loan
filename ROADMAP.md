# Project Roadmap

This is a pointer to the main phased implementation roadmap for the `lending-nelson` project.

Please refer to the detailed document:
[Roadmap (docs/planning/ROADMAP.md)](file:///d:/Development/lending_nelson/docs/planning/ROADMAP.md)

## Summary of Phases

- **Phase 0 & 1:** Foundation and Documentation (Current)
- **Phase 2 & 3:** Flutter foundation, design system, and navigation setup
- **Phase 4:** Authentication & Authorization
- **Phase 5 & 6:** Borrower management and loan product config
- **Phase 7 to 9:** Loan applications, approvals, and repayment scheduling
- **Phase 10 & 11:** Payments, receipts, penalties, and collections
- **Phase 12 to 14:** Documents, notifications, offline sync, and reports
- **Phase 15 & 16:** Web admin panel and n8n workflow integrations
- **Phase 17 to 20:** Security hardening, QA, release prep, and deployment
